## 2. Product Warranty & Guarantee Policy

### 2.1 30-Day Money-Back Guarantee
All new hardware purchased directly from the Acme online store or authorized retail partners is backed by a **30-Day No-Questions-Asked Money-Back Guarantee**. Customers may return undamaged products in their original packaging within 30 days of the delivery date for a full refund.

### 2.2 2-Year Limited Hardware Warranty
Acme hardware devices include a **2-Year Limited Hardware Warranty** starting from the date of purchase.
* **Covered:** Manufacturing defects, sensor calibration errors, wireless module failures, and internal power supply faults.
* **Not Covered:** Cosmetic wear and tear, accidental drops or physical impact, liquid damage exceeding IP67 rated limits, unauthorized opening/repairs, or installation of third-party custom firmware.

## 3. Returns, Refunds & Replacement Process (RMA)

### 3.1 Step-by-Step Return Authorization
To initiate a return or warranty replacement:
1. Log into the customer portal at `https://account.acmesmarthome.example/returns` or email `support@acmesmarthome.example.com`.
2. Provide your original order number, device serial number, and a brief description of the issue.
3. Upon approval, a **Return Merchandise Authorization (RMA)** number and a prepaid shipping label will be sent to your registered email within 24 hours.

### 3.2 Refund Timelines & Shipping Costs
* **Refund Processing:** Refunds are issued within **3 to 5 business days** after our logistics center receives and inspects the returned item. Refunds are credited back to the original payment method.
* **Return Shipping Fees:** 
  * **Defective / Warranty Items:** 100% Free return shipping provided by Acme.
  * **Buyer's Remorse Returns (within 30 days):** A flat return shipping fee of **$4.99** will be deducted from the total refund amount.

## 4. Device Troubleshooting & LED Indicator Status Codes

### 4.1 Acme Smart Hub LED Status Codes
The status light ring on top of the Acme Smart Hub indicates system status:
* **Solid Green:** Device is connected to the internet and operating normally.
* **Blinking Amber:** Wi-Fi connection lost or weak signal strength (below -75 dBm).
* **Solid Red:** Critical hardware error or failed firmware update.
* **Blinking Blue:** Pairing mode active (Ready to connect new sensors).

### 4.2 Hard Factory Reset Procedure
If your Smart Hub becomes unresponsive or displays a **Solid Red** LED:
1. Ensure the hub is plugged into wall power.
2. Locate the small pinhole reset button on the back panel next to the Ethernet port.
3. Insert a paperclip and press and hold the button for **12 seconds**.
4. Release the button when the LED ring flashes **Purple three times**.
5. Wait 2 minutes for the hub to reboot into factory default setup mode.
